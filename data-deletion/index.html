Data Deletion Request

QarzDaftar — Personal Debt & Loan Tracker
Developer: Islomov Odilxon Rustamovich

QarzDaftar stores all your data locally on your device and optionally on your own Google Drive. We do not store any user data on our servers. You have full control over your data and can delete it at any time.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Delete Data Within the App

Step 1. Open QarzDaftar on your device.
Step 2. To delete individual items: go to any account or transaction, tap on it, and use the delete option.
Step 3. To delete all accounts and transactions: delete each account from the accounts list — this will also remove all associated transactions.

Option 2: Clear All App Data via Device Settings

Step 1. Open your device Settings.
Step 2. Go to Apps → QarzDaftar.
Step 3. Tap Storage → Clear Data. This deletes all local data including accounts, transactions, settings, and photos.

Option 3: Uninstall the App

Uninstalling QarzDaftar from your device will permanently remove all local data, including accounts, transactions, photos, templates, and settings.

Option 4: Delete Google Drive Backups

If you used the Google Drive backup feature, a copy of your data may exist on your Google Drive. To delete it:

Step 1. Open Google Drive on your computer or phone.
Step 2. Find the QarzDaftar folder.
Step 3. Delete the folder and empty the Trash.
Step 4 (optional). Revoke the app's access to your Google account at Google Account Permissions.

What Data Is Deleted

Data Type Deleted When Retention After Deletion
Accounts (names, phone numbers) Deleted in-app, cleared data, or uninstalled Immediately and permanently deleted
Transactions (amounts, dates, notes) Deleted in-app, cleared data, or uninstalled Immediately and permanently deleted
Photo attachments Deleted in-app, cleared data, or uninstalled Immediately and permanently deleted
App settings & preferences Cleared data or uninstalled Immediately and permanently deleted
Google Drive backups Manually deleted from Google Drive Subject to Google Drive retention policies
Subscription/purchase data Managed by Google Play Subject to Google's retention policy

Note: If you used the Shared Ledger feature, your partner may have a copy of shared transaction data on their device or Google Drive. Deleting your data does not delete the copy on your partner's device.
